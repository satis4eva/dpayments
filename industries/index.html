Who we help

Payments set up for the way your sector trades.

A café, a plumber and an online shop all take cards — but they need very different setups. Here’s how we typically approach each, and what we pay close attention to.

Retail & shops Cafés, restaurants & bars Trades, builders & merchants Hotels & accommodation Automotive Garden centres & large retail Online competitions & prize draws Ecommerce Law firms & professional services Health, beauty & wellness Events & markets Gyms, clubs & memberships Travel & transport

Retail & shops

Fast queues, clean reconciliation and a setup that copes with Saturdays.

Typical setup

  • Countertop or EPOS-integrated terminals
  • Portable devices for queue-busting on busy days
  • Payment links for click-and-collect or phone orders
  • Online checkout if you sell through a web shop

What we look at closely: Transaction volume and average basket size — interchange-plus pricing often suits shops with lots of small debit card sales.

Cafés, restaurants & bars

Take payment at the table, handle tips fairly and turn tables faster.

Typical setup

  • Portable pay-at-table terminals
  • Bill splitting and tipping with staff reporting
  • EPOS integration so orders and payments match
  • Deposits and pre-orders by payment link

What we look at closely: Tip handling, Wi-Fi coverage across the floor and peak-time reliability.

Trades, builders & merchants

Get paid on the doorstep or over the trade counter, not 30 days later.

Typical setup

  • 4G mobile terminals that work on site
  • Payment links sent straight after a job
  • Virtual terminal for phone payments and deposits
  • Pre-authorisation for larger jobs

What we look at closely: Signal on site, offline mode, larger trade-counter transactions, and avoiding long rental terms.

Hotels & accommodation

Guest payments from booking to check-out.

Typical setup

  • Pre-authorisations at check-in
  • Deposits and balances by payment link
  • Restaurant and bar charges via ePOS
  • Online booking checkout and multi-currency for overseas guests

What we look at closely: Pre-auth handling, no-show policies, future-dated bookings and DCC for international guests.

Automotive

Garages, MOT centres, servicing and dealerships.

Typical setup

  • Countertop terminals at reception
  • Payment links when the car is ready
  • Higher-value transactions and deposits
  • Integration with garage management software

What we look at closely: Larger ticket sizes, deposits on parts and vehicles, and commercial card fees from fleet customers.

Garden centres & large retail

Busy weekends, big catalogues and café counters.

Typical setup

  • Multiple tills with ePOS integration
  • Portable terminals for outdoor areas
  • Separate café and retail reporting
  • Loyalty schemes for regulars

What we look at closely: Seasonal peaks, Wi-Fi coverage outdoors, and running retail and hospitality side by side.

Online competitions & prize draws

Payments for competition and prize draw sites.

Typical setup

  • Hosted checkout with 3D Secure 2
  • Apple Pay and Google Pay for quick entries
  • Fraud screening and spend controls
  • Card payouts for cash prizes

What we look at closely: Your legal structure (free entry route or licence), chargeback rates and partner appetite for the sector.

Ecommerce

A checkout that converts, with fraud and chargebacks kept in check.

Typical setup

  • Hosted checkout or platform plugins
  • Apple Pay and Google Pay for mobile shoppers
  • 3D Secure 2 and fraud screening
  • Multi-currency for international sales

What we look at closely: Underwriting requirements for your site, chargeback rates, reserves and settlement speed.

Law firms & professional services

Solicitors, accountants, consultants, agencies and other client-based firms.

Typical setup

  • Invoice payment links and pay-now buttons
  • Recurring billing for retainers
  • Virtual terminal for client calls
  • Integration with invoicing and accounting tools

What we look at closely: Higher-value transactions, commercial card fees, client vs office account routing for law firms, and getting paid on time.

Health, beauty & wellness

Salons, barbers, clinics, therapists and studios.

Typical setup

  • Countertop or portable terminals at reception
  • Deposits to reduce no-shows
  • Payments linked to booking software
  • Packages and courses billed in instalments

What we look at closely: No-show protection, booking integration and keeping costs low on smaller tickets.

Events & markets

Street food, pop-ups, festivals, fairs and market stalls.

Typical setup

  • 4G mobile terminals with long battery life
  • Offline mode for crowded networks
  • Multiple devices on one account
  • Short-term or seasonal options

What we look at closely: Connectivity in busy venues, battery life and avoiding monthly minimums in quiet months.

Gyms, clubs & memberships

Sports clubs, gyms, schools, societies and subscription businesses.

Typical setup

  • Recurring card payments on a schedule
  • Card updater to reduce failed payments
  • Online sign-up with payment
  • In-person terminals for classes, kit and bar sales

What we look at closely: Failed payment recovery, churn and simple reporting for committees or managers.

Travel & transport

Taxis, private hire, tour operators, coach hire and hospitality stays.

Typical setup

  • Mobile terminals for in-vehicle payment
  • Deposits and balances by payment link
  • Pre-authorisation for hire and damage holds
  • Online booking checkout

What we look at closely: Future-dated bookings can mean extra underwriting checks or reserves — we’ll explain what to expect up front.
