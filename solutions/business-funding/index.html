Business funding

Funding that flexes with your card sales.

A merchant cash advance gives you a lump sum upfront, repaid automatically as a small percentage of your future card takings. Busy months repay faster; quiet months repay less. We introduce you to specialist funding partners.

  • No fixed monthly repayments
  • Introduced via funding partners
How it works

Simple, sales-linked funding.

Upfront funds

A lump sum based on your card turnover history, to use for stock, refits, equipment or cash flow.

Repaid as you trade

An agreed percentage of each day’s card takings goes towards repayment.

Fixed total cost

You agree one fixed fee upfront, so you know exactly what you’ll repay.

Flexes with your sales

Repayments rise and fall with turnover, easing pressure in quiet periods.

Questions

Good to know.

More answers in our full FAQ, or just ask us.

Who is eligible?

Typically UK businesses that have taken card payments for several months with consistent turnover. Each funding partner sets its own criteria.

Is DPayments the lender?

No. Funding is provided by third-party funders, subject to status, affordability and their terms. We may receive a commission for introductions.

Is a cash advance a loan?

A merchant cash advance is usually structured as the purchase of a share of your future card sales rather than a loan, but read each funder’s terms carefully and take independent advice if unsure.

DPayments is not a lender and does not provide financial advice. Funding is offered by third-party providers, subject to status, eligibility and their terms and conditions. We may receive a commission from funding partners for introductions.
