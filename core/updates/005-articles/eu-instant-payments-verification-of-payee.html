Sending euros across Europe used to mean waiting until the next business day. The EU's Instant Payments Regulation (Regulation (EU) 2024/886) is changing that, and it has added an important anti-fraud check along the way.

What the regulation requires

  • Ten seconds, 24/7. Instant euro credit transfers must complete within ten seconds, every day of the year.
  • No premium pricing. Banks can't charge more for an instant transfer than for a standard one.
  • Verification of Payee (VoP). Before a payment is authorised, the payer's provider must check that the payee's name matches the IBAN and warn the customer if it doesn't. This applies to all euro credit transfers, not just instant ones, and must be free of charge.

The timeline

For banks in the euro area, two deadlines have already passed: receiving instant payments from 9 January 2025, and sending them, together with VoP, from 9 October 2025. Further deadlines are still ahead:

  • Payment and e-money institutions must be able to receive instant euro payments from January 2027.
  • Providers outside the euro area face their own 2027 deadlines for sending and receiving instant euro payments.
  • Bulgaria, which joined the euro in 2026, has until 1 January 2027 to meet the instant-payment, equal-charges and VoP obligations.

Is it working?

A year in, the consumer name check is working, though unevenly: many corporates are opting out of VoP for bulk payments. Fraud is the reason the check matters. A joint EBA and ECB report found that fraud on instant payments grew 175% over three years, against 98% growth in transaction volumes.

What businesses should do

  • Make sure your account name matches. Customers paying you will now see a warning if the name on their payment doesn't match your account. Put your exact legal account name on invoices and checkout pages.
  • Use instant payments for cash flow. Faster settlement from customers and to suppliers can free up working capital.
  • Check your provider's readiness. If you use a payment or e-money institution for euro accounts, ask about their January 2027 plans.
