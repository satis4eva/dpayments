Worth understanding

Two ways to handle currency.

Multi-currency pricing (MCP)

You price in the customer’s currency and the card is charged in that currency. Conversion happens on your side at settlement, so you control the rate and the customer sees a clean, local price.

Dynamic currency conversion (DCC)

The customer is offered the choice of paying in their home currency at the point of sale. It’s common on terminals for tourists. It must be optional and clearly shown, and the rate is often less favourable for the customer.

Questions

Do I need a bank account in every currency?

No. You can convert to your home currency at settlement. If you have costs in another currency, settling into a matching account can save you a second conversion.

Which currencies can I accept?

It depends on the provider, but all the major currencies are widely supported. Tell us which markets you sell into and we’ll check coverage.

Is multi-currency more expensive?

International cards usually carry higher interchange whatever currency you charge in. What varies most between providers is the FX margin, which is why we compare it.
