How fast are card payouts?

Many eligible cards receive funds within minutes. Some issuers take longer; the provider can tell you what to expect by card type and country.

Can I pay out to any card?

Most Visa and Mastercard debit cards are eligible, and many credit cards too. Coverage varies by issuer and country.

Is this the same as a refund?

No. A refund reverses a previous payment. A payout is a new transfer, which lets you send amounts that differ from the original deposit, such as winnings or profits.
