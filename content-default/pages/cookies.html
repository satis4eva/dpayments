Cookie policy

Effective 25 July 2026

The short version: we don’t currently set any advertising or analytics cookies, and we don’t track you across other websites. If that changes, we’ll update this policy and ask for your consent where the law requires it.

What cookies are

Cookies are small text files a website stores on your device. They can help a site work, remember your preferences or measure how it’s used.

What we use

We only use strictly necessary cookies that keep the site secure and working properly. These are essential and don’t require consent under UK rules. We do not use analytics, advertising or social-media tracking cookies. Our pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when your browser requests them; no cookies are set for this.

Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies through your browser settings. Because we don’t use non-essential cookies, blocking them won’t stop you reading the site or sending an enquiry.
