Can withdrawals go back to cards?

Yes. Card payouts let you return funds to the card a client deposited with, which is often required for AML reasons.

Do you work with offshore brokers?

We work with brokers regulated in recognised jurisdictions. Partner appetite varies, so tell us where you’re licensed.

DPayments is an independent payments partner. We introduce merchants to regulated acquirers, PSPs and specialist partners that work with this sector. We only work with appropriately licensed businesses, and approval, pricing and reserves depend on each partner’s underwriting.
