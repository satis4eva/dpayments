Why it matters

The real cost of fraud is more than the sale.

Chargeback fees

Each dispute usually carries a fee on top of the lost sale and the goods or service already delivered.

Monitoring programmes

Card schemes monitor chargeback and fraud ratios. Exceeding thresholds can mean fines, reserves or losing your account.

False declines

Over-blocking turns away genuine customers. Good fraud tools protect revenue as well as reduce losses.

Network tokens

Replace stored card numbers with scheme-issued tokens.

Network tokens are issued by Visa, Mastercard and other schemes to stand in for a customer’s real card number. They’re tied to your business and update automatically when a card is replaced or expires.

Higher approval rates

Issuers trust tokenised transactions more, which can lift approvals on repeat and subscription payments.

Fewer failed renewals

Tokens update when cards are reissued, so subscriptions keep running.

Less sensitive data

A stolen token is useless outside your business, reducing breach risk.

Potentially lower costs

Some schemes and acquirers offer better pricing on tokenised transactions.

Questions

Good to know.

More answers in our full FAQ, or just ask us.

Does 3D Secure hurt conversion?

Older versions did. 3DS2 uses risk data so most customers are approved without a challenge, and those who are challenged usually confirm in their banking app.

What chargeback rate is too high?

Thresholds vary by scheme and change over time, but as a rule of thumb, staying well below 1% of transactions is important. Specialist sectors are watched more closely.

Can you help if I already have a chargeback problem?

Yes. We’ll look at where disputes are coming from and recommend fixes — from descriptors and refund policy to screening rules and alerts.
