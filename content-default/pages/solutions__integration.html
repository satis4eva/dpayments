Questions

Good to know.

More answers in our full FAQ, or just ask us.

Which integration has the least PCI work?

Payment links and hosted payment pages, because card data never touches your systems. A full server-to-server API carries the most responsibility.

Can you build the integration for us?

Our team at DBS Group builds websites and apps, so if you need development help we can scope it with you.

Do I need a developer for a plugin?

Usually not. Most ecommerce plugins can be installed and configured by anyone comfortable with their store’s admin panel.
