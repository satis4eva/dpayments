Why open banking for investments?

It suits larger payments: lower costs than cards on big amounts, instant confirmation and no card-style chargebacks.

Do partners work with new platforms?

Some do, depending on your regulatory status, ownership and business plan. We’ll tell you who is likely to support you.

DPayments is an independent payments partner. We introduce merchants to regulated acquirers, PSPs and specialist partners that work with this sector. We only work with appropriately licensed businesses, and approval, pricing and reserves depend on each partner’s underwriting.
