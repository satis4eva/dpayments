Questions

Good to know.

More answers in our full FAQ, or just ask us.

Does offering buy now, pay later cost more?

BNPL providers usually charge the merchant a higher fee than cards, in exchange for paying you upfront and carrying the repayment risk. It can pay off on higher-value items.

Can I take PayPal through my card provider?

Many gateways offer PayPal as an option on the same checkout and reporting, so you don’t need a separate integration.

Is open banking cheaper than cards?

Often, yes, especially for larger payments. But not every customer is comfortable with it yet, so it works best alongside cards.
