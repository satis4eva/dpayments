How smart routing works

The right provider for every payment.

Instead of sending every transaction to one acquirer, an orchestration layer decides in real time where each payment should go.

1

Payment arrives

Card type, country, currency, amount and risk score are read instantly.

2

Rules are applied

Route by cost, approval rate, currency or region — or let performance data decide.

3

Best provider chosen

The payment goes to the acquirer most likely to approve it at the best cost.

4

Automatic retry

If a soft decline comes back, it can be retried through another provider in milliseconds.

Questions

Good to know.

More answers in our full FAQ, or just ask us.

Do I need orchestration?

Not always. For a single shop or modest online volume, one good provider is simpler. Orchestration pays off as volume, markets and the cost of downtime grow. We’ll tell you honestly which camp you’re in.

Will I need to rebuild my checkout?

Usually you integrate once with the orchestration platform, and adding providers afterwards is configuration rather than development.

Does orchestration replace my acquirer?

No. It sits in front of your acquirers and PSPs. You still need merchant accounts with the providers it routes to — which we can help arrange.

DPayments is an independent payments partner. We arrange these services through regulated acquirers, payment service providers and specialist partners; availability, pricing and approval depend on your business, sector and the partner’s underwriting.
