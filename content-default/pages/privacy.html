Privacy notice

Effective 25 July 2026

This notice explains how DPayments handles the information you share with us through dpayments.co.uk. We aim to collect as little as possible and to be clear about how we use it.

Who we are

DPayments operates this website and provides payments consultancy and setup services for UK businesses. You can contact us about anything in this notice at {{email}}.

What we collect

We only collect information you choose to give us, mainly when you send an enquiry or request a quote:

  • Your name and email address
  • Your business name, phone number, type of business and approximate card turnover, if you provide them
  • How your business takes payments and what you’d like a quote for
  • Anything you include in your message, or in documents (such as merchant statements) you send us by email

We never ask for card numbers or payment credentials through this site. Please don’t send them to us by email.

Why we use it

We use your information to respond to your enquiry, prepare a quote or statement review, and continue the conversation you started with us.

Our lawful basis

Under UK GDPR, we rely on our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries about our services. Where relevant, we also process your information to take steps at your request before entering into a contract.

Who we share it with

We use trusted third-party providers for website hosting, storing enquiry details and delivering email. They process data on our behalf, under contract, and only as needed to provide their service. If you ask us to arrange a payments service for you, we will share relevant details with the provider(s) you choose, with your agreement.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry information only for as long as we need it to handle your enquiry and for a reasonable period afterwards, then delete it. You can ask us to delete it sooner.

Cookies

See our cookie policy. In short, we only use strictly necessary cookies.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct, delete or restrict your personal data, and you can object to certain processing. Email {{email}}. If you’re unhappy with how we’ve handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this notice

We may update this notice from time to time. The effective date at the top shows when it last changed.
