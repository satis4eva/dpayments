Questions

Good to know.

More answers in our full FAQ, or just ask us.

Which coins and networks are supported?

USDT, USDC, ETH, BNB and TRX across TRC20, ERC20 and BEP20. Ask us if you need others.

Does DPayments hold my funds?

No. DPayments acts as an introducer only. You contract directly with the payment provider, and we never hold, receive or transfer client funds.

Can I also take card payments?

Yes. Many merchants combine crypto with cards, wallets and open banking. See our iGaming and solutions pages.

DPayments acts as an introducer only. Crypto payment services are provided by our licensed gateway partners, and merchants contract directly with the payment provider. DPayments does not hold, receive or transfer client funds. Cryptoassets are high risk and unregulated in some jurisdictions; their value can go down as well as up.
