Questions

Good to know.

More answers in our full FAQ, or just ask us.

Can I keep my existing ePOS?

Often, yes. Many ePOS systems integrate with several payment providers. We check which providers your system supports before recommending anything.

Is ePOS hardware bought or rented?

Both models exist, and software is usually a monthly subscription. As with card machines, we set out the full cost and contract terms.

Do I need integrated payments?

For busy venues, it saves time and errors. For a small shop with one till, a standalone card machine can be simpler and cheaper.

DPayments is an independent payments partner. We arrange these services through regulated acquirers, payment service providers and specialist partners; availability, pricing and approval depend on your business, sector and the partner’s underwriting.
