Questions

Good to know.

More answers in our full FAQ, or just ask us.

Do you work with unlicensed operators?

No. Every partner we introduce requires a valid licence for the markets you operate in.

Why do gaming merchants need more than one acquirer?

Acquirer appetite for gaming changes, and a single provider is a single point of failure. Orchestration across several providers protects your approval rates and keeps you trading.

Will there be a reserve?

Often, especially for new operators. The size and duration depend on your history and the partner. We get it disclosed and negotiated up front.

DPayments is an independent payments partner. We introduce merchants to regulated acquirers, PSPs and specialist partners that work with this sector. We only work with appropriately licensed businesses, and approval, pricing and reserves depend on each partner’s underwriting.
