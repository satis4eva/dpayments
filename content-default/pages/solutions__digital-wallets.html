What you can offer

Every way to pay without typing a card number.

Apple Pay

Accept payments from iPhone, Apple Watch and Mac with Face ID or Touch ID — contactless in store and one-tap on your website or app.

Google Pay

Let Android and Chrome users pay with saved cards in a tap, in store or online.

Click to Pay

The card schemes’ standard for online checkout. Returning shoppers pay with cards saved to their Visa, Mastercard or Amex profile — no long card form.

Contactless in store

Any modern terminal accepts phone and watch payments, just like a contactless card.

Stronger security

Wallet payments use device tokens and biometric checks, so the real card number is never shared with you.

Better conversion

Fewer fields means fewer abandoned baskets, especially on mobile.

Questions

Good to know.

More answers in our full FAQ, or just ask us.

Do wallet payments cost more than cards?

Generally no. A wallet payment is still a card payment, priced at the underlying card’s rate.

Do I need a new card machine?

Not if it’s contactless. Phone and watch payments work on any modern contactless terminal.

Is Click to Pay the same as Apple Pay?

No. Apple Pay and Google Pay are device wallets. Click to Pay is a card-scheme service that lets shoppers use cards saved to their scheme profile on any device or browser.
